## Leave types and yearly allowance
There are four leave types. Casual leave: 12 days per calendar year. Sick leave: 10 days per year. Earned leave: 15 days per year. Unpaid leave: unlimited. Used days are approved requests in the current year; pending requests reserve days until they are decided. You cannot request more days than are available for that type; choose fewer days or Unpaid leave instead.

## Working days, weekends and public holidays
Leave is counted in working days, Monday to Friday, including both the start and end date. Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays are not counted. A request that falls only on weekends or holidays is not needed and is rejected. The HOD maintains the public holiday calendar on the Holidays page.

## Long leave, supporting documents and two-level approval
Leave of more than 3 working days is long leave. Long leave requires at least one supporting document, for example a medical certificate or a travel booking (PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC or DOCX, up to 5 MB each, up to 5 files). Long leave needs approval from the reporting manager first and then the Head of Department (HOD). If your reporting manager is the HOD, a single HOD approval covers both levels.

## Approval workflow and status
Short leave (3 working days or fewer) needs only your reporting manager's approval. Statuses: Pending (waiting for the current approver), Info requested (the approver asked a question; reply on the request page and it goes back to the same approver), Approved, Rejected (always with a comment explaining why) and Cancelled. Only the current approver can act, and nobody can approve their own leave.

## Half-day leave
A single-day request can be a full day, the first half or the second half of the day. A half day counts as 0.5 working days. The first half and the second half of the same date can be requested separately. Half days cannot be used for multi-day requests.

## Backdating, advance planning and request length
Leave can be backdated by up to 30 days, for example sick leave already taken. Leave can be planned up to one year in advance. A single request can cover at most 60 calendar days; split longer absences into separate requests. A new request cannot overlap your other pending or approved leave.

## Editing and cancelling
You can edit a request while it is pending or waiting for information. Saving changes restarts the approval from level 1 so every approver sees the final details. You can cancel a pending request at any time, and an approved request until the leave starts.

## Privacy, audit and notifications
Every sign-in, decision and document view is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log that the HOD can review. Documents are visible only to you and your approvers. You get an in-app notification when your request is approved, rejected or needs more information.
